Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding pricing, timber selection, revisions, and international freight.

How are custom wood carving commissions priced?
Pricing is determined by three factors: the species and rarity of the hardwood timber, the overall scale (surface area and thickness), and the level of relief depth (flat bas-relief versus multi-plane undercut carving). We provide an all-inclusive transparent quote following our initial sketch discussion.
Can you carve from photographs or existing architectural motifs?
Yes. We frequently carve custom bas-relief portraits from family photographs, recreate historical heritage moldings, and interpret sacred symbols from temple blueprints with exquisite precision.
How long does a typical commission take?
Standard bespoke reliefs (under 3 feet) take 3 to 5 weeks. Monumental friezes, full-size entry doors, or complex freestanding sculptures take 6 to 12 weeks. We schedule each commission carefully to ensure undivided attention.
Do you ship custom carvings internationally?
Yes, we ship worldwide. Every carving is packed in fumigated, moisture-sealed museum-grade timber crates with shock-dampening interior molds and full insurance coverage.
What care is required for hand-carved hardwood?
Hardwood pieces require minimal maintenance. Keep them away from direct continuous heating vents or torrential water. Dust gently with a soft camel-hair brush, and apply our complimentary organic beeswax balm once a year to preserve the rich amber patina.
