Frequently Asked Questions

Carving Commissions & Studio Inquiries

Everything you need to know about our commissioning process, hardwood timbers, timelines, and worldwide shipping.

What is the typical timeline for a custom wood carving?
Small to medium sculptural carvings and wall medallions typically require 2 to 4 weeks. Monumental architectural panels, fretwork screens, and complex multi-figure reliefs take 6 to 10 weeks depending on complexity, timber drying, and multiple coats of hand-buffed wax.
Which hardwood species do you recommend for indoor vs outdoor?
For outdoor and moisture-exposed areas, seasoned Burma Teak is ideal due to its natural silica and oils. For interior wall art and sculptures, American Walnut and Indian Rosewood (Sheesham) provide breathtaking color contrasts and intricate grain detail.
Can I approve the design sketches before carving begins?
Yes, absolutely. We begin with a 1:1 scale charcoal sketch and dimensional elevation. Carving only commences once you have reviewed and signed off on the sketch and chosen timber block.
How are delicate carvings packed for worldwide transit?
Each piece is wrapped in breathable archival tissue, cushioned inside high-density foam molds, and crated in custom reinforced plywood cases with moisture desiccants to ensure pristine worldwide arrival.
Can I visit the workshop in Chennai to see work in progress?
We welcome patrons and interior designers by appointment from Monday to Saturday. Please send us an enquiry or call in advance so our master carvers can guide you through the studio.
