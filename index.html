Conscious Materiality

Sustainably Sourced Hardwood Species

We work exclusively with certified, responsibly harvested timber celebrated for its resonant grain, density, and enduring grace.

Dense & Weatherproof

Burma Teak

Rich in natural silica and essential oils, teak yields a warm golden hue that resists humidity and develops an incomparable silken lustre over decades.

Ideal for: Architectural doors & outdoor panels
Deep Contrast

American Walnut

Renowned for chocolate-brown tones with subtle purple undertones and flowing cathedral grain patterns, carving crisply under sharp gouges.

Ideal for: Sculptural objects & wall art
Heirloom Density

Indian Rosewood (Sheesham)

An exceptionally heavy, tight-grained hardwood showcasing alternating streaks of golden amber and espresso, perfect for intricate filigree and figurines.

Ideal for: Fine figurine carving & keepsakes
Distinct Medullary Rays

Quarter-Sawn Oak

Characterized by striking ray fleck patterns and remarkable structural integrity, offering an organic architectural quality that patinates gracefully.

Ideal for: Heritage friezes & furniture accents
