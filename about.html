The Atelier

A lifelong reverence for the grain.

Every tree carries a lifetime of weather, wind, soil minerals, and seasonal rhythms embedded within its growth rings. When timber arrives in our workshop, we don't force a preconceived blueprint onto it—we study its movement, density, and natural flow.

We work exclusively with certified reclaimed and sustainably harvested hardwoods, including Burma Teak, American Walnut, Indian Rosewood, and Quarter-Sawn Oak. Our studio preserves centuries-old Indian and classical European carving traditions while creating sculptural heirlooms tailored for modern architectural spaces.

Our Workshop Principles

Four Tenets of Slow Woodcraft

How we ensure every commission achieves heirloom permanence.

01 • Timber First

Aged Seasoning

We work only with timber seasoned for a minimum of 24 months to prevent checking, warping, or seasonal shrinkage.

02 • Hand Gouges

No CNC Machines

Every contour is guided by eye and carved with hand chisels, leaving subtle faceted tool marks that reflect authentic maker presence.

03 • Non-Toxic

Natural Finishes

We avoid synthetic polyurethane. We nourish the grain with pure cold-pressed tung oil, orange oil, and organic beeswax.

04 • Provenance

Ethical Sourcing

All timber is sustainably sourced from regulated farm forestry and architectural salvage with complete origin traceability.
